StarPilot constellation data

Constellation stick figures adapted from Modern by the Stellarium team.
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/daace2add6a1bf886e8ee1934f51e9c69f818d18/skycultures/modern

J2000 star coordinates from the Astronexus HYG Database v3.5.
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database

Traditional Chinese star names from Stellarium Chinese sky culture v26.3,
contributed by Karrie Berglund and Sun Shuwei, matched by Hipparcos ID.
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/blob/v26.3/skycultures/chinese/description.md

The derived data contains indexed, deduplicated segments and their endpoints.
It is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The manual star-hop supplement selects additional Hipparcos stars of
apparent magnitude 4.0 or brighter from HYG v3.5 and matches available
Chinese names from Stellarium v26.3. It uses the same attribution and
CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.
